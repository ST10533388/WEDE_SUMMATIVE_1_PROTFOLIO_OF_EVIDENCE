About Our Organisation

Discover the origin story, core framework, and curators behind the network.

Our Roots & Sourcing History

Founded by passionate collectors in 2025, Mangaverse Collectibles established direct connections with licensed Tokyo distributors to completely eliminate counterfeit products from the domestic hobby market. By cutting out secondary brokers, we ensure every scale model statue, graphic light novel, and display katana arrives with a certified chain of custody.

Our Mission

To supply high-quality, verified pop-culture items with total transparency and community-driven customer service. We aim to shield local hobbyists from inflated shipping rates and unverified market variants.

Our Vision

To become the premier trusted retail destination for authentic anime products across the country, cultivating a safe space where collectors can effortlessly track, request, and acquire pristine merchandise.
